## Block 10 — Verbatim Q&A Pairs

---

**Q1: Is JEE mandatory for B.Tech admission at ITS Engineering College?**

No, JEE Main is not mandatory. ITS Engineering College accepts JEE Main scores through UPTAC counselling, but you can also get admission through CUET, the college's own entrance exam (ITSEE), or even on the basis of your Class 12th PCM percentage.

---

**Q2: What is the minimum percentage required for B.Tech?**

You need a minimum of 45% marks in Class 12th with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. For students from SC, ST, OBC, or EWS categories, the minimum is 40%.

---

**Q3: What is the total fee for B.Tech CSE at ITS?**

The total fee for B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering is Rs. 5,29,000 for the complete 4-year duration. Year 1 is Rs. 1,39,000 and Years 2, 3, and 4 are Rs. 1,30,000 each. Additional one-time charges include registration (Rs. 1,500), exam fee (Rs. 7,500 per year), and uniform (Rs. 7,500).

> ⚠️ Always follow up with: *"We also have merit scholarships and fee waivers that can significantly reduce this amount."*

---

**Q4: Is ITS Engineering College affiliated to AKTU?**

Yes. ITS Engineering College, Greater Noida is affiliated to Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, and is approved by AICTE, New Delhi.

---

**Q5: Is ITS Engineering College government or private?**

It is a private, self-financed institution. However, it is affiliated to AKTU, which is a government university — so your degree is issued by AKTU, which is a recognized government university.

---

**Q6: What is the highest placement package at ITS Engineering College?**

The highest package offered is Rs. 49 LPA. The average package is Rs. 4.5 LPA. More than 111 companies participated in the last placement cycle, and over 142 offers were made. Top recruiters include Wipro, HCL, IBM, Capgemini, Cognizant, Genpact, and Tech Mahindra.

---

**Q7: Does ITS Engineering College have hostel facilities?**

Yes, the college has separate hostel facilities for male and female students. The hostels are equipped with Wi-Fi, a mess facility, and recreational areas.

---

**Q8: How many campuses does ITS have?**

ITS – The Education Group has 4 campuses: Greater Noida, Mohan Nagar in Ghaziabad, Murad Nagar, and the Ghaziabad UG Campus. Across these 4 campuses, there are 8 institutions offering over 20 programmes.

---

**Q9: What B.Tech branches are available at ITS Engineering College?**

The available B.Tech branches are: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Civil Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE), and Mechanical Engineering.

---

**Q10: When was ITS Engineering College established?**

ITS Engineering College, Greater Noida was established in 2006. The parent group — I.T.S The Education Group — was founded in 1995 by Dr. R.P. Chadha.

---

**Q11: Is there a scholarship for JEE toppers?**

Yes. Students with a JEE Main percentile of 90 or above receive a significant merit waiver on their fees — this can be up to approximately Rs. 40,000 off the first year. Additionally, students who score 80% or above in semester exams receive Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 15,000 off their next year's fees.

---

**Q12: What is the MBA fee at ITS Engineering College?**

The total MBA fee for the 2-year programme is Rs. 2,57,000. Each year is approximately Rs. 1,28,500. Admission is based on CAT, MAT, or CMAT scores, and a management quota is also available.

---

**Q13: What accreditation does ITS Engineering College have?**

ITS Engineering College is accredited by NAAC with a B++ grade and by NBA. It is approved by AICTE, affiliated to AKTU, recognized by DSIR and CSIR, and has an IIC-recognized innovation centre. The parent group — ITS The Education Group — holds an NAAC A+ grade.

---

**Q14: How do I apply to ITS Engineering College?**

You can apply online at itsengg.edu.in. For B.Tech, the main route is through JEE Main + UPTAC counselling. You can also apply directly using the college's own entrance exam or your 12th marks. You can also call our toll-free number 1800 309 5855 for step-by-step guidance.

---

**Q15: Does ITS Engineering College accept CUET?**

Yes, CUET scores are accepted for B.Tech admission at ITS Engineering College as an alternative to JEE Main.

---

**Q16: What is the reservation policy at ITS Engineering College?**

Yes, the college follows the Government of India reservation norms. Reserved categories include SC, ST, OBC, EWS, and PwD candidates. The minimum eligibility percentage for these categories is 40% in Class 12th (vs 45% for general category).

---

**Q17: Is there a lateral entry B.Tech programme?**

Yes. Diploma holders can apply for direct 2nd year entry through B.Tech Lateral Entry. The programme duration is 3 years and the total fee is Rs. 2,82,000. Admission is through UPTAC lateral entry counselling.

---

**Q18: What is the NIRF ranking of ITS Engineering College?**

ITS Engineering College is ranked in the 151 to 300 band under the Innovation category by NIRF 2023. It is ranked 245 by India Today, 168 by Outlook, and 152 by Times of India in 2025.
